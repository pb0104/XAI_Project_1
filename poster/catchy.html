Whose Side Is Your AI On?

What if a store quietly pays to promote some laptops? Would the AI still look out for you?
Pranshul Bhatnagar · Duke University
What we foundA store can push the AI, and you often can’t tell.Saying “sponsored” and the AI’s own explanation did not protect the shopper.

How we tested it
