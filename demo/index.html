    What this shows

    • It’s easy to push an AI. One hidden sentence from the store raised how often it picked the sponsored laptop from 21% to 55%.
    • It’s hard to notice. The word “sponsored” and the AI’s own explanation did not reliably show the push or protect you.
    • Don’t let it check itself. Compare with your own limits, and ask for the best non-sponsored option.
